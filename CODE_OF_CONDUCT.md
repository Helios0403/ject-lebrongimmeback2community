# Ject Justice Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Ject Justice community to be a respectful and safe place for everyone. Participation is welcome regardless of race, colour, ethnicity, national origin, caste, immigration status, language, disability, neurodiversity, body size, age, religion, socioeconomic status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, sex characteristics, family status, or experience level.

Ject Justice explicitly affirms LGBTQIA+ inclusion and Black Lives Matter. This fork is dedicated to the memory of George Floyd.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Treat people with dignity and respect.
- Use welcoming, inclusive language and listen to people affected by a decision.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop harmful behaviour.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, deliberate misgendering, racist or anti-LGBTQIA+ conduct, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behaviour violates these commitments. Report concerns privately to the maintainers of the fork; do not post personal information or allegations publicly unless the affected person chooses to do so.

This code applies in project spaces and at project-related events.
